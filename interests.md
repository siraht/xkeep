# Interests

- Software, open-source projects, developer tools, hardware, security, science, engineering, and product releases
- Business, startups, markets, platforms, policy, media, design, and cultural developments with concrete information
- Firsthand observations, substantive arguments, useful recommendations, strong explainers, and genuinely novel ideas
- Interesting or surprising items are welcome even when they are not globally important or immediately actionable
